## How to Add Videos

To add a new video to this list:
1. Copy the template below
2. Replace `VIDEO_TITLE` with your video title
3. Replace `VIDEO_ID` with your YouTube video ID
4. Add a brief description

```markdown
### VIDEO_TITLE
- **URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID)
- **Description:** Brief description of your video
```
