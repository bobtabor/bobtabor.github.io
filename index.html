"Happy trails to you, until we meet again"

Hey friends, I started LearnVisualStudio.NET back in 2002 in response to expensive week long training courses. I never thought it would amount to much. No one else I knew was doing video tutorials back then except Lynda.com. I was shocked as anyone that the idea had legs. And I was able to keep it going for over 16 years! But YouTube, a thousand competitors and now AI made it difficult to continue. So, I'm officially calling it quits. If you've been following along, this shouldn't come as a surprise since I haven't posted new videos in years. I always thought, "maybe, someday ..." But now I don't write code daily anymore (sad, I know), and AI can do a better job at teaching than I ever could. So, it's time to wrap it up and say goodbye. We had a good run. Thanks for being part of it.
